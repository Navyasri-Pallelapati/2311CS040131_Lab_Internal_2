Vector databases store high-dimensional embeddings and support efficient
approximate nearest neighbor (ANN) search. FAISS, developed by Meta AI,
supports exact search (IndexFlatIP, IndexFlatL2) as well as approximate
indexes like IVF and HNSW for large-scale retrieval. Cosine similarity is
commonly used when embeddings are normalized to unit length, because in
that case the inner product between two vectors equals their cosine
similarity. Chunk size and overlap are important hyperparameters: smaller
chunks improve retrieval precision but may lose surrounding context, while
larger chunks preserve context but can dilute the embedding with irrelevant
information.
